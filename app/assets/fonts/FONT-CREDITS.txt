Fonts used by Gold Explorer (subset to Latin letters, digits and common punctuation)

  serif-*  Gold Serif  = subset of TeX Gyre Pagella  GUST Font License (free to use and to embed)   https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre
  sans-*   Gold Sans   = subset of Carlito           SIL Open Font License 1.1                       https://github.com/googlefonts/carlito
  mono-*   DejaVu Sans Mono                          Bitstream Vera / DejaVu licence (free to use)    https://dejavu-fonts.github.io

The sans and serif files are subsets of the original fonts (glyphs removed, nothing else changed). Their internal names were changed
to Gold Sans and Gold Serif, because the Carlito licence reserves the name "Carlito" for unmodified copies and the GUST licence asks
that modified copies take another name. The copyright lines stay in the files.
The licence texts are in web/licenses (published with the site).
The three families are registered in pubspec.yaml as GoldSerif, GoldSans and GoldMono.
